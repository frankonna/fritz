Education Finance

This loan is to support members take care of their education expenditures;
school fees of their children, tuition for their personal studies, training and
progress. Subject to accessibility of fund, a non-member may take loan but
the loan must be guaranteed by a member of the cooperative. This loan
shall be paid directly to the intended school/institution's Bank Account and
settlement shall be by regular deduction. Tenor and conditions of academic
loans vary on individual basis.
